## Questions & Answers

### Weather
September in Warnkenhagen typically has mild autumn weather (12-20°C / 54-68°F). We recommend bringing a light jacket just in case of rain, and perhaps a warm sweater for the evenings.

### Children
Children are absolutely welcome! We've planned activities to keep them entertained. Please let us know the number and ages of your children when you RSVP.

### What to Wear
Smart casual to semi-formal attire is recommended. Think dresses, nice pants, blazers, and polished shoes. Please avoid very casual clothing like jeans and t-shirts. Given the season, layering is a good idea!

### How to Get There
**By Car:** The venue is easily accessible by car with ample parking available.

**By Train:** The nearest train station is Bad Sobernheim station, about 15 minutes from the venue by taxi. Regional trains connect well to major cities.

**By Plane:** Frankfurt International Airport is about 1.5 hours away by car. We can help arrange transportation if needed!

We'll provide detailed directions and transportation information closer to the date!
