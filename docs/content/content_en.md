## Questions & Answers

### Weather
June in Bad Sobernheim typically has mild to warm weather (15-25°C / 59-77°F). We recommend bringing a light jacket just in case of rain.

### Children
Children are absolutely welcome! We've planned activities to keep them entertained. Please let us know the number and ages of your children when you RSVP.

### What to Wear
Smart casual to semi-formal attire is recommended. Think dresses, nice pants, blazers, and polished shoes. Please avoid very casual clothing like jeans and t-shirts.

### How to Get There
**By Car:** The venue is easily accessible by car with ample parking available.

**By Train:** The nearest train station is Bad Sobernheim station, about 10 minutes from the venue by taxi.

**By Plane:** Frankfurt International Airport is about 1.5 hours away by car.
