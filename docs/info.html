About Our Venue

We're celebrating at the Alte Schule in Klutzer Winkel – a place we absolutely love and think is worth exploring! The venue is run by Barbara and Karl Decker, and Karl happens to be Felix's godfather. We've visited this special place several times and have come to really appreciate it. We think if you make the journey all the way out here for our wedding, you'll love it too!

Klutzer Winkel is a wonderful area situated right by the former border between East and West Germany. There's so much to discover here – beautiful landscapes, charming villages, and fascinating history. If you can, we'd highly recommend staying a few extra days, renting a bike, and exploring the region. It's a truly special corner of the world!

Learn more about the Alte Schule: decker-luebeck.de

Dress Code

We're planning an outdoor celebration, so please dress comfortably for the weather. We suggest elegant casual attire. Since we'll be celebrating by the sea, feel free to bring a swimsuit, also because we might have a sauna for those who want to use it. As there is some natural terrain at the venue, we recommend you bring a pair of comfortable shoes.

Getting There

Our wedding will take place at Alte Schule in Warnkenhagen, Germany. The venue is easily accessible by car from both major German cities and the Danish coast. If you need assistance with travel arrangements or directions, please don't hesitate to contact us. Public parking is available near the venue.
